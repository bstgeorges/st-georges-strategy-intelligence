This week's watch

Four UK consultation closes fall within the next 30 days: captive insurance on 14 October, equity market transparency on 16 October, and FCA and PRA consultations on 22 October. CP26/30 also addresses resilient market operation during outages. The EU AI Act's Article 50(2) transition and new prohibitions apply from 2 December 2026. DORA has applied since 17 January 2025; no later DORA milestone falls in this 90-day window. Later confirmed dates are EIOPA's Solvency II application on 30 January 2027 and an HKMA target date on 1 March 2027.
