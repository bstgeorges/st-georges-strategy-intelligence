Weekly Judgement

Week ending 20 Sept 2026 · Edition 14

The conditions are the control.

Archive record · The Weekly Judgement published with this edition is retained here in full.

What happened

Anthropic is putting independent evaluators inside its model-development process. The SEC has set conditions for tokenised-stock venues, OSFI has published its 2027 market-risk chapter, and CISA has added newly exploited vulnerabilities.

Why it matters

These are not broad policy tests. They bind a real activity to particular conditions: who can see the evidence, whether the underlying asset and rights match, how code is auditable, which data supports a risk measure, and how quickly a known exposure can be found.

What to do

Choose one live external platform or market workflow. Write down the two or three conditions that make it acceptable, name the evidence that shows they still hold, and take any breach — not a generic assurance update — to the next risk committee.

The weekly readout

Conditions, not comfort

The useful leadership question is not whether a proposal was approved. It is whether the few conditions that make it acceptable still hold in live operation.

01 / What happened

Specific conditions are moving into the foreground.

Anthropic is embedding evaluators; the SEC has set conditions for tokenised-stock trading; OSFI has published its 2027 market-risk chapter; and CISA has added exploited flaws.

02 / Why it matters

The permission lives in the detail.

Access, rights equivalence, auditable code, reliable data and a known estate are not attachments to a control. They are the conditions that make the activity defensible.

03 / What to do

Name the conditions.

Choose one live platform or workflow. State the two or three conditions that make it acceptable and the evidence that proves they hold now.

04 / Evidence

Bring the exception, if there is one.

Take a breached condition to committee with the source record, current fact pattern and a clear decision on whether the activity can continue.

Reading the signals

Where the condition sits

Three short read-across points for the next leadership conversation.

Independent scrutiny

Access determines whether assurance can be challenged.

An evaluator who cannot see the relevant safeguards, evidence and operating context cannot test a provider’s account of its own system.

Source / Anthropic, 18 Sept

Conditional activity

A route to market can be conditional throughout its life.

For tokenised stock, rights, code, disclosure and market safeguards have to remain aligned; the original approval is only the starting point.

Source / SEC, 17 Sept

Time-bound evidence

Evidence has to arrive at the speed of the risk.

Model and data implementation plans, and emergency patching, fail in the same place when the current estate cannot be found and evidenced in time.

Source / OSFI, 10 SeptSource / CISA, 18 Sept

Executive challenge

Three questions from the week

Carry one into a committee, 1:1 or control review.

  1. Which conditions make this platform, model or market route acceptable — and where is the live proof?
  2. Who has enough access and independence to challenge that proof when the arrangement changes?
  3. What would tell us that a condition has failed, and who decides whether the activity pauses or continues?
