The Daily Intelligence Briefing reports a G7 agreement to run tabletop exercises for AI-enabled cyberattacks and Bank of England work with banks on independent, clean-system recovery. Together, these offer a possible through-line from collective agent behaviour to response and recovery across shared financial infrastructure. Tuesday’s reported supervisory questions add context but are secondary reporting. The 9 October brief had already emphasized authority persistence, independent enforcement, traceability and containment; this week’s angle should move beyond those controls to correlated behaviour, shared dependencies and recoverability.

Possible editorial test: **Can the firm detect and contain harmful interactions among agents, and restore critical services if an AI-enabled attack compromises shared infrastructure or the normal control plane?** Keep this distinct from Edition 17’s transcript and incident-record test. Do not imply that Project Agenti is a supervisory requirement, that G7 exercises have happened, or that a new binding requirement exists.

Before promotion, verify Project Agenti on the BIS site and identify whether it is a research simulation, an active experiment or a published result. Verify the G7 announcement with the relevant official finance-ministry or chair statement and the Bank of England statement or speech. Confirm the precise scope of the exercise, who is expected to participate, and what “independent” or “bare-metal” recovery means in the source. Use Reuters only as corroboration where official material is available.
