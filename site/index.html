The Judgement

The AI evidence base has to cover both conduct and resilience

The FCA’s retail-AI review and the ESAs’ systemic-cyber warning are not separate stories. Together, they raise the standard for proving that AI is controlled before, during, and after it acts.

What changed
AI supervision is becoming more operational: customer outcomes, model dependency, cyber exposure, and third-party concentration now sit in the same control conversation.
Why it matters
A policy statement is not enough. Committees need to see authorisation paths, monitoring evidence, stop controls, recovery playbooks, and accountable owners.
The decision test
If an AI-enabled workflow causes harm or accelerates an incident, can the firm explain what happened, stop it, recover service, and evidence who owned each decision?

Committee Question

Three questions to use this week

Short enough to lift into a committee pack; specific enough to force evidence rather than reassurance.

AI governance

Can we stop an AI-enabled workflow quickly, prove why it acted, and show who owned the decision?

A strong answer should include permissions, monitoring, override, rollback, and accountable-owner evidence.

Cyber resilience

Have our cyber scenarios been refreshed for AI-accelerated attack speed and model-enabled social engineering?

A strong answer should connect threat intelligence, incident rehearsal, identity controls, and service recovery.

Customer outcomes

Where AI changes a customer decision or journey, what evidence shows the outcome remains fair and explainable?

A strong answer should show data provenance, testing, complaints insight, exception handling, and control ownership.

Open Committee Questions

What to Ask For

The evidence request is the product

The point is not more commentary. It is getting from public signal to the internal evidence a senior leader should ask an owner to produce.

  • AI inventory and permissionsWhich workflows use AI, who authorised them, what can they access, and where is the human override?
  • Stop and recovery pathHow quickly can an agentic workflow be paused, reversed, and communicated if it creates customer or operational harm?
  • Scenario refreshWhich cyber, fraud, resilience, and third-party scenarios changed because AI alters attack speed, dependency, or decision scale?
  • Customer-outcome testingWhat shows the AI-enabled journey remains fair, explainable, monitored, and complaint-ready?
